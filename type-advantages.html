Type 3 of 5 - Purple Advantages

Advantages Outweigh: Work From Home

EXAM Q: Working from home has risen. Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?
Plan: verdict disadvantages win. Body1 pros short, Body2 cons longer. Same verdict intro body conclusion.

Intro

S1 Paraphrase
Remote working has become widespread in recent years.
WHY: working from home to remote working, has risen to become widespread. Short paraphrase enough.
S2 Thesis
Although it offers flexibility, I believe drawbacks outweigh benefits due to isolation and lost teamwork.
WHY: Although balances then verdict outweigh. Names two reasons promised.
S3 Outline
This essay will discuss both sides before arguing negatives dominate.
WHY: Roadmap plus stance dominate.

Body 1 - Advantages short

S1 Topic
One major advantage is schedule freedom and no commute.
WHY: Template One major advantage is. Two nouns one idea freedom.
S2 Explain
This means employees save time and balance family duties.
WHY: This means shows result. Links real life.
S3 Example
For instance, a UK survey found remote staff saved 70 minutes daily.
WHY: Survey plus number proves claim.
S4 Result
As a result, job satisfaction initially rises.
WHY: initially hints benefit fades, prepares Body2 turn.

Body 2 - Disadvantages stronger

S1 Topic
However, home work weakens collaboration and mentorship.
WHY: However, turns. Same theme teamwork.
S2 Explain
This is because spontaneous chats that spark ideas rarely happen online.
WHY: Explains why online less creative. rarely adds precision.
S3 Example
For example, Microsoft reported weaker team ties after long remote periods.
WHY: Company name adds weight.
S4 Extend
Furthermore, juniors struggle to learn without observing seniors.
WHY: Furthermore, second disadvantage. Makes Body2 longer equals verdict.
S5 Judge
Hence, productivity losses exceed time savings.
WHY: Direct outweigh judgement with Hence,. Answers question words.

Conclusion

S1 Restate
In conclusion, despite flexibility, I contend disadvantages of remote work prevail.
WHY: contend for believe, prevail for outweigh. New words show LR.
S2 Close
Therefore, firms should adopt hybrid models rather than full remote.
WHY: Balanced solution hybrid, not new argument. About 275 words.
