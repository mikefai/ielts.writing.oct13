Type 4 of 5 - Orange Problem

Problem: Traffic Congestion

EXAM Q: Traffic congestion is growing in cities. What are causes and solutions?
Plan: Body1 two causes, Body2 two solutions matching 1-to-1. Matching = CC score.

Intro

S1 Paraphrase
Urban traffic jams have intensified in recent decades.
WHY: congestion to jams, growing to have intensified. Present perfect shows trend.
S2 Thesis
This essay will outline car dependence and poor transit as causes and propose charges and upgrades.
WHY: Names 2 causes plus 2 solutions. Examiner checks bodies match list.
S3 Outline
First causes will be discussed before effective measures are suggested.
WHY: Passive will be discussed formal. Maps order.

Body 1 - Causes

S1 Cause 1
Firstly, private car ownership has surged due to rising incomes.
WHY: Cause plus reason in one topic sentence.
S2 Explain
This means more vehicles compete for limited road space.
WHY: This means shows effect logically.
S3 Cause 2
Secondly, public transport remains unreliable in suburbs.
WHY: Second cause same para allowed. Secondly, orders.
S4 Example
For instance, in Manila commuters wait 40 minutes, so they prefer cars. Consequently roads clog.
WHY: City + number proves unreliable. so plus consequently double link.

Body 2 - Solutions match

S1 Solution 1
To tackle car overuse, cities should impose congestion charges.
WHY: To tackle links back to cause 1. should gives solution voice.
S2 Explain
This is because fees discourage trips and fund transit.
WHY: Double benefit discourage plus fund. Matches cause logic.
S3 Solution 2
In addition, investing in metro lines offers alternative. For example, Seoul cut jams after expanding subways.
WHY: In addition, second solution matches cause 2. Example proves works.
S4 Result
As a result, commuters shift to trains and roads clear.
WHY: Shift to trains fixes car dependence named in intro. Full circle high CC.

Conclusion

S1 Restate
In conclusion, car reliance and weak transit cause jams, yet pricing and investment can relieve them.
WHY: Summarises both causes plus both solutions one sentence. yet contrasts.
S2 Close
Therefore, municipal action now will prevent gridlock worsening.
WHY: Future warning. No new cause. About 270 words.
