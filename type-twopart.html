Type 5 of 5 - Rose Two-part

Two-part: Rural Youth Leave

EXAM Q: Many young people leave rural areas for cities. Why? Is it positive or negative?
Missing one Q = TR 5. Plan: Body1 Why jobs plus study, Body2 negative verdict.

Intro

S1 Paraphrase
Rural-to-urban migration among youth has accelerated.
WHY: leave rural for cities to migration. young people to youth. Noun phrase shows LR.
S2 Thesis both
This essay will explain job and study motives and argue trend is largely negative for villages.
WHY: Must answer Why plus positive/negative in thesis. Names motives plus verdict.
S3 Outline
First reasons will be explored before negative impacts are assessed.
WHY: Passive explored assessed formal. Promises Body1 reasons Body2 judgement.

Body 1 - Why

S1 Reason 1
Primarily, cities offer better paid jobs and career growth.
WHY: Primarily, first reason. Answers Why directly.
S2 Explain
This is because factories and services cluster in urban hubs.
WHY: Economic logic cluster. TR depth.
S3 Reason 2
Moreover, universities concentrate in cities. For instance, 80 percent of top colleges sit in capitals.
WHY: Moreover, second reason same Why. Percent proves.
S4 Result
Consequently, ambitious youth migrate to access work and study.
WHY: Consequently, sums both reasons.

Body 2 - Positive or Negative

S1 Verdict
Although migrants gain income, I consider exodus harmful overall.
WHY: Although admits upside then verdict harmful. Answers second Q.
S2 Explain
In other words, villages lose labour and age rapidly.
WHY: Explains harm lose labour plus age. Strong nouns.
S3 Example
For example, abandoned farms in Spain and Japan show rural decline.
WHY: Two countries prove global pattern.
S4 Extend
Furthermore, overcrowded cities face housing strain.
WHY: Adds city harm too. Balanced negative case.

Conclusion

S1 Restate both
In conclusion, youth move for jobs and education, yet I believe depopulation makes trend negative.
WHY: Repeats Why plus verdict one sentence. yet contrasts.
S2 Close
Therefore, governments should invest rurally to retain talent.
WHY: Solution retain talent. No new cause. About 272 words.
