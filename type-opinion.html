Type 1 of 5 - Blue Opinion

Opinion: Tech + Communication

EXAM Q: Technology has made life more complex but communication easier. To what extent do you agree?
Signal: To what extent = pick side. Plan: strongly agree. Body1 concession short, Body2 strong longer.
IntroBodyConclusionConnector

Intro - 3 sentences

S1 Paraphrase
It is often argued modern technology complicates life though it simplifies interaction.
WHY: No copy. complex to complicates, communication easier to simplifies interaction. Template It is often argued proves understanding.
S2 Thesis
In my opinion, I strongly agree digital tools eased communication although add complexity.
WHY: Direct answer to To what extent. Previews concession with although so Body1 expected.
S3 Outline
This essay will acknowledge complexity before explaining connectivity benefits.
WHY: Maps Body1 then Body2. Formal will. Never I will talk about.

Body 1 - Concession short

S1 Topic
Admittedly, constant notifications can overwhelm users.
WHY: Admittedly, fairly shows opposite side. One idea only.
S2 Explain
This is because people feel pressure to reply instantly.
WHY: Answers Why. This explaining sentence lifts TR 6 to 7.
S3 Example
For instance, office staff check messages 80 times daily, causing stress.
WHY: Number 80 makes proof real. For instance, template. No example = abstract max Band 6.
S4 Result
As a result, some feel life is more hectic.
WHY: Closes loop with As a result,. Keep short so weak side does not win.

Body 2 - Strong side longer

S1 Topic
However, video calls have revolutionised family and business contact.
WHY: However, pivots. Strong verb revolutionised shows LR.
S2 Explain
In other words, distance no longer blocks daily contact or remote work.
WHY: Restates simply. Shows paraphrase skill.
S3 Example
For example, Zoom let rural students continue classes in pandemic.
WHY: Real event + group = strong TR.
S4 Extend
Moreover, free apps save money versus international calls.
WHY: Moreover, adds second benefit same idea.
S5 Verdict
Therefore, accessibility gains far exceed notification stress, which settings can limit.
WHY: Compares sides, sets up conclusion verdict.

Conclusion

S1 Restate
In conclusion, despite minor complexity, I firmly believe tech greatly eased communication.
WHY: Paraphrase thesis. despite + noun shows grammar range. No copy.
S2 Close
Therefore, people should learn digital balance rather than reject tools.
WHY: Recommendation. No new argument. About 275 words.
Reuse skeleton: It is often argued... In my opinion I strongly agree... This essay will... Admittedly... This is because... For instance... As a result... However... In other words... For example... Moreover... Therefore... In conclusion...
