02. Each Sentence

INTRO 3 sentences

S1 Paraphrase. It is often argued that + [own words]
WHY: university free to tertiary without charge. No copy proves comprehension.
S2 Thesis. In my opinion, I strongly agree... / While both views have merit, I believe...
WHY: Direct answer. No thesis = Band 5 TR.
S3 Outline. This essay will first discuss X before arguing Y.
WHY: Roadmap Body1 then Body2.

BODY twice

S1 Topic. Firstly, / Secondly, / One major benefit is...
WHY: One paragraph one idea or CC falls.
S2 Explain. This is because... / This means that...
WHY: Answers Why How. Lifts TR 6 to 7.
S3 Example. For instance, / For example,...
WHY: No example = abstract max Band 6.
S4 Result. As a result, / Consequently,...
WHY: Closes loop. Next para starts However.

CONCLUSION 2

S1 Restate. In conclusion, although X, I believe Y...
WHY: Paraphrase thesis. Do not copy.
S2 Close. Therefore, governments should...
WHY: Recommendation. No new idea.
